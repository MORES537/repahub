History, culture and community of Cuban Reparto music

What is Reparto?

Reparto is an urban music genre born in Cuba in the late 1990s, fusing reggaeton with Cuban timba, rap, and electronic music. It originated in the popular neighborhoods of Havana and is characterized by direct lyrics, accelerated rhythms, and an overwhelming energy that connects with everyday Cuban reality.

CubaCuban ReggaetonTimbaCuban TrapUrban

History of the Genre

Emerging in the late 90s in East Havana — especially in Alamar, Regla, and Guanabacoa — Reparto developed a unique Cuban identity distinct from Puerto Rican reggaeton. It incorporates elements of rumba and street culture. Through the 2000s, artists brought it to national and international audiences.
